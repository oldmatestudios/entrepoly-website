The Problem

Scaling Quality Learning Is Hard

01

More Learners = More Trainers

The moment you try to scale learning experiences to 100s or 1000s, you need more people to deliver them.

02

More Trainers = More Cost

More trainers means more time, more costs, and more complexity. Margins shrink fast.

03

Training Orgs Fail To Scale

All this leads to training organizations failing as they try to grow beyond their physical capacity.

"The same training as a live experience, but minus the additional cost of more trainers and extra overhead."

Our Solution

What Happens When 1000s Play Our Game?

Learners take on the role of a business owner, making decisions and getting feedback from the game.

This is the same training as a live experience — same skills, same pressure, same outcomes — but minus the additional cost of more trainers and extra overhead.
